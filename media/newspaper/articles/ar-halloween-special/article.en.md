# Augmented Reality: When a Ghost Suddenly Appears Next to You 👻

You point your phone camera at an empty spot. There's nothing there.  
Then you look at the screen – and suddenly, right in front of you, there's a ghost.

Phone down: gone.  
Phone up: there again.

What sounds like a pretty good Halloween scare is called **Augmented Reality**, or **AR** for short.

---

> Augmented Reality means "extended reality."  
> Your phone doesn't just show you the real surroundings captured by the camera.  
> It adds digital things on top – for example, figures, animals, arrows, text. Or a ghost.

> Maybe you already know AR without knowing the name.  
> Some apps let virtual figures appear in your room. Others show you through the camera what a piece of furniture would look like in your home. Some games use AR too.

## How does the ghost know where to stand?

For this, AR needs an **anchor point**.

This could be a specific image, a sign, or a symbol.  
Once the software recognizes this image through the camera, it knows: something should happen here.

It can then place a ghost right next to a landmark, for example.  
Move your phone, and your view of the ghost changes too.  
On screen, it looks as if the digital figure is truly part of the surroundings.

## And how does AR get onto the phone?

These days it works **without any special app**.

You open a link or scan a QR code. It opens a website and asks for access to your camera. The website contains the prepared AR scene.

**Here's what happens technically:**

→ Software recognizes the surroundings or a specific image

→ digital figure is inserted at the defined position

→ both appear together on your screen.

---

## Can I make something like this myself?

**Yes.** With simple WebAR tools it's possible even without coding:

- You choose an image or location as a trigger.
- You upload your digital figure and set where it should appear.
- The tool then creates a link or QR code.
- When someone scans it and points the camera at the right trigger: boom, your AR figure appears.
